## About
AI adoption, supplier reviews and audit requests create a common problem: the facts are scattered, ownership is unclear and decisions stall.

I build connected governance workflows that bring AI inventories, supplier dependencies, control evidence and review gates into one decision process. My public workspace lets you inspect the calculation rules, change records and trace findings to a management decision.

Engagements focus on three outcomes:
• AI governance: a defined inventory, evidence requirements, oversight responsibilities and release-review memo.
• SaaS and AI vendor assurance: documented data-handling risks, supplier dependencies and treatment decisions.
• Audit readiness: linked control evidence, accountable action owners and a clear review agenda.

For a scoped sprint, we agree the evidence, outputs and acceptance criteria before work begins. I am open to remote GRC roles and consulting engagements.
