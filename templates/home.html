Operating approach

Governance that
behaves like a system.

I build connected governance workflows for AI risk, supplier assurance, technology risk and control evidence. The goal is simple: make every material conclusion traceable to a source fact, reviewer and next action.

Current focus includes AI inventories and lifecycle oversight, NIST AI RMF and NIST AI 600-1 mapping, post-deployment drift and material-change review, third-party AI and LLM risk, ISO/IEC 42001, SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR processor governance and EU AI Act transparency/classification review.

Scoped services

Small surface.
Concrete outputs.

Each engagement starts with one decision boundary and ends with reviewable deliverables, not an open-ended advisory retainer.

AI governance review sprint

Bound the AI inventory, oversight roles, evaluation evidence, release gates and post-deployment drift/change review. Outputs: system register, prioritized gaps and decision memo.

AI vendor & supplier assurance

Review SaaS, LLM and external AI vendors across data use, subprocessors, assurance evidence, dependency risk and accountable treatment. Outputs: reviewed register, evidence requests and remediation plan.

Continuous assurance review

Refresh control evidence, expiry, exceptions and retest ownership on an agreed cadence. Outputs: updated register, exception agenda and management summary.
