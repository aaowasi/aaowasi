From scope to handover

  1. Define the decision

    Set the boundary, material risks, accountable owner and acceptance criteria.

  2. Review the evidence

    Separate verified observations, missing evidence, exceptions and assumptions. Preserve source dates and review rationale.

  3. Own the treatment

    Record the decision, action owner, due date and evidence required for closure. Escalate exceptions against agreed authority.

  4. Maintain the system

    Reassess material changes, retest controls and hand over editable records with explicit calculation definitions.

Before we begin

What will I receive?

An agreed register, evidence-gap list and decision memo with owners, next actions and acceptance criteria.

How are scope and price agreed?

Share a short brief. We confirm inputs, deliverables, review responsibilities, timeline and commercial terms before starting.

Can my team maintain the outputs?

The handover includes editable records, calculation notes and a walkthrough. Any follow-up support is agreed separately.

Can I evaluate this for a remote role?

Yes. Review the professional profile and governance workflows, then use the contact brief to share the role and interview timeline.
