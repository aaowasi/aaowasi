Executive bio

Abdullah Al Owasi builds governance workflows that connect supplier, AI, privacy and control evidence to accountable decisions. His independent portfolio brings ten disciplines into one working risk workspace, with traceable scoring, explicit review gates and exportable management decisions.

Core competencies

  • AI inventory, oversight, transparency and evaluation governance
  • Supplier due diligence, processor obligations and dependency risk
  • Enterprise risk prioritization, treatment decisions and management reporting
  • Control testing structure, evidence freshness and remediation review
  • Versioned data contracts, deterministic decision logic and validation

Demonstrated work

Ten connected modules share one browser-local workspace. The implemented contract supports up to 250 records and 10 MB imports. Thirteen JavaScript core tests cover interoperability, scoring, validation and export safety; five existing Python engine tests pass.

Independent portfolio work uses synthesized scenarios and public reference taxonomies. Scope, methods and source evidence are available for inspection.

Services

AI governance sprint

Define a bounded use-case inventory, oversight responsibilities, evaluation evidence and release-review gates. Deliverables: system register, gap priorities and a decision memo. Scope and acceptance criteria agreed before work begins.

Supplier & assurance sprint

Structure vendor intake, processor obligations, control evidence and risk treatment for an agreed service set. Deliverables: reviewed register, evidence requests and owner-led remediation plan.

Recurring governance review

Support an agreed monthly review of risk decisions, evidence expiry and open actions. Deliverables: refreshed register, exception agenda and management summary. Cadence, access and reviewer responsibilities agreed per engagement.
