Profile

I build connected GRC workflows that turn AI, supplier, privacy and control evidence into accountable decisions. My public portfolio combines ten governance modules with traceable scoring, explicit review gates, browser-local data handling and exportable decision outputs.

What I optimize for

For teams reviewing AI, suppliers or assurance gaps, the useful output is not another static checklist. It is a reviewable next action: what is known, what is missing, who owns the decision and what evidence closes it.

Core competencies

  • AI inventory, NIST AI RMF / Generative AI Profile mapping, oversight, transparency and evaluation governance
  • Supplier due diligence, processor obligations and dependency risk
  • Enterprise risk prioritization, treatment decisions and management reporting
  • Control testing structure, evidence freshness and remediation review
  • Versioned data contracts, deterministic decision logic and validation

Demonstrated work

Ten connected modules share one browser-local workspace. The implemented contract supports up to 250 records and 10 MB imports. Thirteen JavaScript core tests cover interoperability, scoring, validation and export safety; five existing Python engine tests pass.

The public workspace uses synthetic scenario data and public reference taxonomies so the decision logic, guardrails and evidence boundaries can be inspected without exposing client or confidential records.

Services

AI governance review sprint

Bound the AI inventory, oversight responsibilities, evaluation evidence, release-review gates and post-deployment change review. Deliverables: system register, prioritized gaps and decision memo.

AI vendor & supplier assurance sprint

Structure vendor intake, processor obligations, control evidence and risk treatment for an agreed service set. Deliverables: reviewed register, evidence requests and owner-led remediation plan.

Continuous assurance review

Review evidence expiry, exceptions, retest ownership and open decisions on an agreed cadence. Deliverables: refreshed register, exception agenda and management summary.
