Profile

I build connected GRC workflows that turn AI, supplier, privacy and control evidence into accountable decisions. The public portfolio is designed for inspection: source records, scoring logic, evidence states, human review gates, tests and decision outputs are visible rather than hidden behind static screenshots.

What hiring teams can verify

  • AI inventory and lifecycle governance, NIST AI RMF / NIST AI 600-1 mapping, human oversight, evaluation evidence and post-deployment monitoring.
  • Third-party and AI vendor risk across SaaS, LLM integrations, data use, subprocessors, assurance evidence and dependency risk.
  • Technology-risk prioritization, risk treatment, exceptions, ownership, KRIs and management decision trails.
  • Audit readiness and continuous assurance: evidence freshness, control-test states, remediation ownership and retest.
  • Clear operating boundaries: automation assists review; legal judgments, audit opinions and material risk acceptance remain human-owned.

Public proof

Ten connected modules share one browser-local workspace, supported by a continuous GRC suite spanning 13 operating domains, 16 shared record types and 103 register/reporting views. Repository tests cover scoring, validation, interoperability and export safety; recorded workflow results link to exact commits.

The public scenarios use synthetic data and public reference taxonomies. They demonstrate methods and implementation—not client deployment, certification, audit opinion or achieved financial impact.

Ways to work together

AI governance review sprint

AI vendor & supplier assurance

Review SaaS, LLM and external AI vendors across data use, subprocessors, assurance evidence, dependency risk and treatment ownership. Outputs: reviewed register, evidence requests and remediation plan.

Continuous assurance review

Review evidence expiry, exceptions, open issues and retest ownership on an agreed cadence. Outputs: refreshed register, exception agenda and management summary.
