A seven-day sprint, with a defined scope

For a bounded review with evidence available at kickoff: days 1–2 establish the inventory and evidence requests; days 3–5 assess gaps and treatment options; days 6–7 deliver the decision memo, owner-led action plan and handover. Confirm the scope and delivery date before booking.

What we agree first

Scope, evidence access, review period, decision owners, required outputs and acceptance criteria. Timing and commercial terms depend on that brief. Legal judgments, audit opinions and client authorization stay with the responsible reviewers.
