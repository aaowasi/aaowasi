Typical bounded sprint

Where the evidence is available at kickoff, a compact review can often fit into a seven-working-day delivery pattern: scope and requests, assessment and treatment, then decision memo and handover. The actual date is agreed only after the inputs and review boundary are confirmed.

What we agree first

Scope, evidence access, review period, decision owners, required outputs and acceptance criteria. Timing and commercial terms depend on that brief. Legal judgments, audit opinions and client authorization stay with the responsible reviewers.
