Agentic GRC / human decision boundary

AI can prepare. Accountability stays human.

Modern GRC agents can gather evidence, draft registers, compare documents and prepare audit work. My operating model keeps the consequential calls with accountable reviewers: scope, materiality, control effectiveness, risk acceptance, exceptions and sign-off.

01 / Agent preparation

Gather, draft and cross-check.

Use automation to reduce clerical friction across evidence requests, registers, policy comparisons and framework preparation.

02 / Evidence assurance

Verify before relying.

Preserve source dates, provenance, completeness, freshness and reviewer rationale before evidence supports a conclusion.

03 / Decision rights

Keep risk calls accountable.

Separate machine recommendations from human authority for effectiveness, residual risk, exceptions, release and acceptance.

04 / Agent governance

Authorize tools, data and change.

Connect identity, evidence provenance, agent/tool/data permissions and ongoing monitoring to explicit escalation and review gates.
