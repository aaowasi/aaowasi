Privacy & data handling

Minimal collection.
Explicit boundaries.

This public site does not include advertising trackers, a server-side contact database or hidden form submission. Theme preference is stored in your browser. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which receives standard connection information, and hosting providers may process ordinary connection logs.

The public governance workspaces are browser-local demonstrations. Do not enter confidential evidence, credentials, raw prompts, personal customer records or data you are not authorized to store on your device.

Contact forms prepare an email in your own email application; nothing is submitted to this site unless you send that email yourself. External services such as GitHub, LinkedIn and hosting providers operate under their own privacy terms.
